What changed, and why — eleven fixes, none to the sidebar

The sidebar is untouched

Label over a one-line hint, six items, identity and connection at the foot. My icon version threw the hints away for scannability nobody asked for. The only change is the active item: the accent on its label, instead of a background 3% lighter than its neighbours.

The blue is gone

The ground was #070b10 — a blue-black. A hue in the ground tints every neutral above it and reads cold. This is #0B0C0E, a true neutral charcoal, so the accent is the only chroma on the page.

Four figures lead, not sixteen

The dashboard gave a headline figure and a supporting detail the same box, border and weight. Now four lead at 31px; the other fifteen become a read-down list where the numbers align and can be compared.

Internal codes off the screen

NEEDS_OCR and EQUATION_PAGES are identifiers for your database, not words for a client. Each alert now says what happened and carries the action that fixes it.

Text that clears AAA

Secondary text was #7d90a4 — 4.1:1, below the accessibility floor for its size. Now 16.4:1 primary, 9.4:1 secondary, 5.3:1 tertiary.

A percentage gets a bar

"94%" is a number. 94% with the remaining 6% drawn beside it is a fact about your corpus you can see at a glance.

Semantic colour, used once

"Needs attention: 2" carries amber on the figure itself — not a coloured box around it. One signal per state, so colour still means something when it appears.

A typeface with a point of view

IBM Plex — Sans for interface, Serif for quoted passages, Mono for every figure, with tabular numerals so columns line up. Open licence, self-hostable: ship the woff2 files and the zero-outbound-request guarantee holds.

Content has a measure

Text ran the full width of a 1900px monitor. Body caps at 64 characters, quotations at 58.

The 75-second wait explains itself

Four named stages, a real elapsed count, a typical range, and one line saying why it is slow. Every stage is reported by the backend when it happens — none is guessed from a clock.

Plain language throughout

"Evidence drift" became "Documents have changed". "OCR" became "recognition", with the acronym on hover. Names things the way the reader would.

Every colour is a token, defined once and redefined for light. Nothing is hard-coded into a component, so changing the accent is one line rather than a search across forty files.
